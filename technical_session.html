Read About

Powering India’s Manufacturing Future with Innovation, Sustainability and Global Excellence

This Technical Summit is Driving Innovation Quality and Global Competitiveness in plastics & Engineering Industries. This Conference Brings together industry Leaders, Technologists, Policymakers, Researchers and manufacturers to explore the future of Engineering, Plastics and advanced manufacturing. Designed as a high-impact knowledge Platform alongside the exhibition, the Conference addresses the most critical themes shaping industry transformation from product design and tooling to Automation, sustainability, circular economy, and Global Trade. As industries move toward Smart manufacturing, Lightweight Materials, Electric Mobility, and Sustainable Production, the need for innovation, Quality Excellence, and Global Integration has never been greater. This Conference offers practical insights, emerging Technologies, and real-world strategies that help businesses especially MSMEs upgrade capabilities, improve Competitiveness, and tap into new market opportunities.

Each Session is Carefully Curated to Deliver a Complete Value Chain Perspective

Enhancing Quality Standards in Automotive Engineering and h3lastic Components. Accelerating Product development using DFMA, Simulation, and Rapid Prototyping. Adopting Smart Automation and Robotics for Next- Generation Factories. Strengthening India’s Tooling Ecosystem for Consistent, High-Quality Moulding. Leveraging Engineering Polymers and Composites for Sustainable Industrial Growth. Building Profitable Models in the Recycling and Circular Economy. Expanding Export Opportunities and Positioning India as a Global Plastics hub. The Conference Serves as a Bridge between Technology Providers, Manufacturers, Researchers, Certification bodies, and Policymakers, Engineering, Plastics encouraging Collaboration and knowledge Exchange that leads to real industrial progress. With expert Speakers, Industry case studies, and Interactive Panel Discussions, this Technical Summit is set to be a Catalyst for Innovation, Sustainability, and Global Competitiveness in the and Engineering and Plastics Sectors.

TECHNICAL SUMMIT TITLES

  • 1) Innovative Product Design and conceptualization from Prototyping to Production –DFMA
  • 2) Automation and Robotics – Driving Next Gen Manufacturing.
  • 3) Mould and Die Tooling in India-Enhancing Moulding consistency challenges and opportunities.
  • 4) Transforming Industries: Engineering Polymers for a Sustainable Future.
  • 5) Recycling and circular Economy: The Next Generation Business Opportunity Strategy.
  • 6) Export and Import possibilities in plastics India’s Global Trade Strategy.
  • 7) Recycling & Circular Economy in the Rubber Industry: Next Generation Business Opportunities and Strategies.

1) Innovative Product Design & Conceptualisation: From Prototyping to Production – DFMA Approach

Synopsis:

Transforming innovative ideas into market-ready products requires seamless coordination between design, validation, and manufacturing. This session explores how smart product design, CAE integration, and DFMA principles reduce cost and complexity while improving manufacturability. Experts will also discuss additive manufacturing, rapid prototyping, and strategies for protecting innovation through patents, copyrights, and trademarks. The session provides a complete roadmap for accelerating product development while ensuring production readiness and commercial success.

    Speaker Topic
  • Smart Product Design with CAE Integration
  • Design for Manufacturing & Assembly (DFMA)
  • Additive Manufacturing & Rapid Prototyping
  • Product Design, Engineering Innovation and IP Protection
    Moderator Topic
  • Integrating Design, Prototyping & Manufacturing for Real- World Production

2. Smart Automation and Robotics – Driving Next Gen Manufacturing

Synopsis:

Manufacturing is undergoing a major transformation driven by automation, robotics, and data-driven intelligence. This session highlights smart factory strategies that integrate machines, sensors, and digital systems to improve productivity and flexibility. Topics include advanced industrial robotics, AI- powered inspection and process control, scalable automation for MSMEs, and the growing role of collaborative robots. The session also addresses workforce transformation and the new skills required in an automated manufacturing environment

    Speaker Topic
  • A. Smart Factory Integration & Digital Manufacturing
  • B. Advanced Industrial Robotics Applications
  • C. AI-Powered Automation & Machine Vision
  • D. Human–Robot Collaboration & Future Skills
    Moderator Topic
  • Orchestrating Intelligent Automation for the Future of Manufacturing

3. Mould & Die Tooling in India: Enhancing Moulding Consistency – Challenges and Opportunities

Synopsis:

Tooling quality forms the backbone of consistent and defect-free moulding operations. This session focuses on precision tool design, advanced machining and finishing technologies, maintenance strategies, and the selection of tool steels and treatments. Industry leaders will discuss how Indian tool rooms can adopt modern digital practices, improve productivity, and meet international quality standards. The discussion will also highlight emerging opportunities for Indian tooling companies in domestic and global markets.

    Speaker Topic
  • A. Precision Tool Design for Consistent Moulding
  • B. High-Accuracy Tool Manufacturing Technologies
  • C. Tool Maintenance & Lifecycle Management
  • D. Future Opportunities for Indian Tooling Industry
    Moderator Topic
  • Strengthening India’s Tooling Ecosystem for World-Class Moulding

4. Transforming Industries: Engineering Polymers & Composite Materials for a Sustainable Future

Synopsis:

Advanced polymer materials are playing a crucial role in building lighter, stronger, and more energy-efficient products across industries. This session explores the evolution from commodity plastics to engineering polymers, high-performance materials, and composite technologies. Experts will discuss how these materials contribute to lightweighting, durability, and improved lifecycle performance while supporting sustainability goals. The session also highlights how composite materials are enabling innovative engineering solutions in automotive, infrastructure, and industrial applications.

    Speaker Topic
  • A. Sustainable Innovations in Commodity Plastics
  • B. Engineering Plastics for Lightweight & Durable Design
  • C. High-Performance & Specialty Polymers in Advanced Applications
  • D. Polymer Composites for Lightweight and Sustainable Engineering
    Moderator Topic
  • Driving Sustainable Transformation through Advanced Polymer Innovation

5. Recycling and Circular Economy: The Next-Generation Business Opportunity Strategy

Synopsis:

The global shift toward a circular economy is opening new business models and technology pathways in plastics and polymers. This session examines advanced recycling technologies, circular product design, biodegradable and bio-based polymers, and the financial and regulatory drivers supporting sustainable growth. Industry experts will share insights on how companies can convert environmental responsibility into profitable opportunities while meeting evolving market and policy expectations.

    Speaker Topic
  • A. Circular Economy Business Models in Plastics
  • B. Mechanical & Chemical Recycling Technologies
  • C. Biodegradable and Bio-Based Polymers: Opportunities & Challenges
  • D. Designing Products for Circularity and Sustainability
    Moderator Topic
  • Turning Sustainability into Strategy in the Circular Economy Era

6. Export and Import Possibilities in Plastics: India’s Global Trade Strategy

Synopsis:

India’s plastics industry is increasingly integrated into global value chains, creating new opportunities for exports and strategic import substitution. This session explores global demand trends, MSME readiness for international markets, and export opportunities in engineering plastics and value-added products. Trade experts will also address compliance requirements, logistics, trade agreements, and sector-specific insights including the toy industry’s export–import landscape.

    Speaker Topic
  • A. India’s Plastics Export Landscape & Global Demand
  • B. Empowering MSMEs for International Markets
  • C. Export Opportunities in Engineering Plastics Products
  • D. Toys Export–Import Dynamics: Opportunities, Compliance and Global Market Access
    Moderator Topic
  • Positioning India as a Global Plastics Hub through Trade and Competitiveness

7. Quality Excellence in Engineering & Plastics Components for the Automotive Industry

Synopsis:

As the automotive sector moves rapidly toward electric mobility, lightweighting, and globally integrated supply chains, quality expectations for engineering and plastic components are rising significantly. This session brings together OEMs, Tier suppliers, testing agencies, and quality experts to discuss global standards, defect prevention strategies, validation protocols, and compliance systems. Participants will gain insights into achieving zero-defect manufacturing, improving process capability, and meeting the evolving demands of automotive customers worldwide.

    Speaker Topic
  • A. OEM Quality Expectations & Supplier Readiness
  • B. Consistent Quality in High-Volume Manufacturing
  • C. IATF, PPAP & Global Automotive Quality Systems
  • D. Testing, Validation & Compliance for Auto Components
    Moderator Topic
  • Driving Quality Excellence in Automotive Engineering & Plastics
